## Scope

Moss uses access controls, protected credentials, security logging and recovery procedures to protect the information it handles. The controls available to an integration depend on its version, configuration and deployment.

Moss-operated services and customer-operated installations have different responsibilities. In a customer-operated installation, the customer manages its runtime and infrastructure; Moss remains responsible for its own release, support and authorized access. Contact us to confirm the boundary for your deployment.

## Security controls

| Area | Approach |
| --- | --- |
| Authentication | JWT and API-key checks on the relevant service routes; Clerk for dashboard identity |
| Authorization | Application, organization and session checks appropriate to the route and data |
| Transport | HTTPS for public service endpoints; other connections follow their deployment configuration |
| Stored credentials | One-way hashes for supported API-key verification; encryption for integration tokens and signing secrets that must be recovered |
| Logging | Selected audit events and minimal operational logs; detailed traffic logging depends on configuration |
| Captured content | Filtering and masking on supported paths; captures can still contain personal data |

Organization-scoped routes do not mean every database query includes an organization filter. Shared knowledge resources, privileged administration and customer-operated stores have their own access boundaries. Assess application access for the actual route and deployment.

## Assurance and privacy

SOC 2 Type II examination and ISO 27001 certification work are in progress. Neither an issued SOC 2 report nor ISO 27001 certification is claimed here. Contact us for the current scope and available assurance material.

Deletion, audit and consent features can support privacy obligations. They do not establish compliance with every GDPR or EU AI Act requirement. Applicable roles, data flows and obligations depend on the intended use and customer agreement.

## Responsible disclosure

Report a suspected vulnerability through the Dashboard or [security@viamoss.ai](mailto:security@viamoss.ai). Avoid sending credentials or customer content in an initial report.
