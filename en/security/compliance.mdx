## Current assurance work

Moss is working toward a SOC 2 Type II examination and ISO/IEC 27001 certification. These are in progress; this page does not claim an issued SOC 2 report or ISO certificate. Contact Moss for the confirmed audit scope, period and current status.

Our information security work covers access, controlled changes, incident response, monitoring, recovery and risk management. The operation and evidence for each control are assessed separately.

## Privacy requests

Where GDPR or other privacy law applies, the relevant controller determines the lawful basis, notices and handling of data-subject requests. For customer-operated deployments, the customer manages its environment; Moss handles any separate support copy it receives according to the applicable agreement.

### Access and portability

Contact Moss or the relevant customer administrator to request access or an export. Available records and export formats depend on the service and deployment. Verify the requester's authority before disclosing personal data or another user's logs.

### Deletion

The user-deletion flow covers primary user records and related session, message, plan and instruction metadata. Knowledge content, uploaded objects, vectors, recordings, audit records, backups and copies held by other services have separate storage and deletion paths. A successful user deletion is not proof that all of those copies have been removed.

For a deletion request, establish its scope, check the relevant stores and any legal preservation duty, then confirm the outcome and any retained records. Apply the applicable contract and privacy notice; do not infer a complete purge from a single API result.

### Lawful basis and agreements

Consent, contractual necessity or legitimate interests may be relevant, depending on the activity and applicable law. An SDK option alone does not establish a lawful basis or valid consent. Contact Moss about the applicable DPA, processing scope, recipients and transfer terms.

## EU AI Act

Applicable duties and risk classification depend on the intended use, deployment and the parties' roles. Moss does not classify every deployment as limited risk or claim universal EU AI Act compliance.

Application owners should make AI use and its limitations clear, provide appropriate human oversight and confirm the event records available for their use case. Available audit events do not establish that every AI interaction is logged.

## Domain restrictions

Where configured, allowed domains restrict SDK initialization. An empty allowlist permits all domains; domain restrictions are an additional control and do not replace authentication.

Configure the deployment's allowed domains in the Dashboard security settings, including the required production and development environments. For example:

```text
app.example.com
staging.example.com
localhost:3000
```
