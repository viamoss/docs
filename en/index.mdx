## Welcome to Moss

Moss is an AI assistant that you embed in your web app. It reads the page your user is on, answers their questions from your documentation, and can walk them through a task by highlighting each element to click.

### What Moss does

- **Answers in context.** Moss reads the text and structure of the page the user is on, with the parts you mark as sensitive redacted, so its answers fit what the user sees. See [Page data and privacy](/en/sdk/page-data).
- **Guides step by step.** Moss highlights the button or field for each step on the real page.
- **Answers from your knowledge base.** Connect your help center or documentation, and Moss cites the articles it answers from.
- **Hands off to support.** Moss drafts a support ticket and files it to Zendesk, either itself or through your own backend.
- **Replays sessions.** With session recording turned on (it is off by default), you can review what users did in the dashboard.

Use the [Moss Dashboard](https://dashboard.viamoss.ai) to:

- create and configure applications;
- manage your knowledge base, procedures and suggested content;
- review sessions, session replays and analytics.
